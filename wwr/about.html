River Run

We live to guide thrill-seekers through nature’s most untamed waters, delivering unforgettable whitewater adventures with a commitment to absolute safety, expert instruction, and pure, unfiltered adrenaline.

History

Founded in 1998 by a group of passionate river guides, our company began with just two rafts and a dream to share the thrill of white water with the world. Over nearly three decades, we have safely guided over 100,000 adventurers down the region's most spectacular rivers, growing into the premier rafting outfitter while maintaining our deep commitment to river conservation and unforgettable outdoor experiences.
